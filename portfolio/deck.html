noesis and Eigenlucy · 2025

Motivation: Neuronal headstages bottlenecked researchers

2025 was full of startup hype about neuron computers. At Frontier Tower's neurotech floor, friends were discussing biological neuronal responses to nootropics and neuropeptides. [ neurotech ]

We tested 3 laser deposition techniques

at atopile's machine shop

1. Most promising: Laser-induced graphene

We directed a laser beam at the polyimide tape, for graphene to become deposited on the surface.

CO2 laser at 10 600 nm, 5.5 W, 88 mm/s scanning rate, 100 lines/cm.

The triple-layered electrode version was consistently conductive. However, the graphene easily wears off, or encounters insulation at corners.

2. Accidental discovery: Aluminum vapour deposition

While implementing laser-induced graphene, we noticed dense metallic stripes appeared on glass--This certainly didn't look like graphene.

This was in fact, the vapour from the underlying sheet of aluminum, in a DPSS laser of 1064 nm.

3. Error-prone: Copper metalization

A focused laser beam mimicks the voltage differential like a battery. As it is directed at the copper solution, it causes a thermal gradient. The heated area gains copper under the plate.

This method has been time-intensive and prone to error.

[ neurotech ]

when the scale started tweaking

preparing the electroplating solution

We became suspicious when the 100 mL of water weighed 125 grams. Consequentially, in our 2nd copper replication attempt, the copper solution instantly precipitated into copper hydroxide--a cloudy, turquoise colour, not what we want! The ideal solution should look transparent, dark blue.

We tried a series of quick troubleshooting e.g. switching power supplies, using different scales, but there weren't matching power supplies of 9V for any other scale in the lab.

Thus, I poured in some ethanol to see how much the scale was off by. From then on, we performed everything according to the scale's offset mass (1.25x).

Surprises in focused ultrasound

early may observelog · 2026

Manual transducer placement was the bottleneck in this study, taking 15 mins on average. Robotic arms are developed for TMS coils e.g. Axilum robotics, but could not generalize to the coregisteration step.

Algernon-Gordon effects remain unstudied. The postdocs weren't sure about how the focused ultrasound stimulation decays as a function of time, since they're focused on the what.

Institutional arbitrage. The publish-or-perish pressure had caused me, an outsider, physical pain.

Wiggling. While I accidentally wiggled in my chair, I felt a phantom cold sensation in my throat. I suspect my vagus nerve was stimulated by accident through the incident beam.

[ autoresearch ]

The rwx cycle

I research in cycles · 2026
Autoresearch weaponization to ingest hundreds of papers in hours, run in-silico experiments, and write up 5000 words in an afternoon when you're too broke to afford journal subscriptions. Tested and true.

r

Literature review

  • Boiling the Ocean method: Cluster these papers into categories to understand "why x hasn't been solved yet".
  • Availability heuristic: look online at your favourite theorists' websites, and see if there are any recent writings that haven't been implemented yet.
  • Then, feed the context into your favourite research agent.

x

Scorched earth hypothesis testing

  • start running experiments ideally on day 1 or 2 to leave time for adjustments.
  • 50% of the time should be spent on running experiments, logistics, or building.
  • Code should be constantly running.
  • Come up with a list of hypotheses (anywhere from 1 to even 40) ranked by upside risk and time taken to test.

w

drafting papers

  • you should be accumulating data points in all sections of the paper in parallel, rather than starting from scratch.
  • Figures drive the narrative.
  • Write these first: Introduction, Related Work, Methods. Write these last: Key contributions (Abstract), Results.

Roman streets · Arxiv wars

2025 – 2026

Roman streets for more tasteful autoresearch

One can use a parallel from Roman street networks since they are engineering spectacles. If some agent has already explored this topic space, streets with wider paths and directions should be constructed to avoid independent and identical exploration by a future agent.

When a connection is bad, roads can be demoted from viae publicae to viae vicinales, marked as dead-ends or topics could be tossed into the sewers. When needed, shortcuts between roads could be created.

Graphs contents are differentiable and trainable. If taking certain routes repeatedly leads to good research, gradient descent can teach the model to prefer these roads.
